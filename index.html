01/ I LIKE

Design
Video Art

Material exploration in Copper and Velvet foil. The »I Like You« chain bridges the digital gap, allowing you to easily share affection in the physical realm. An interactive piece where wearers simply peel off the tactile velvet heart foil to 'like' reality. A commentary on social currency transformed into tangible connection.

02/ BOOBYFUL

Structural Design
Optics & Form

Constructed from Steel and Silver. This modular two-part steel cube system offers 16 distinct combinations, inviting the wearer to find their specific breast shape. A process of translating organic curves into rigid metal. Each resulting form becomes perfectly imperfect, shifting focus to the inherent beauty and individuality of every body type through industrial geometry.

03/ REWORKED

Fabric Metal

Material study: Tin Box. Adopting the mantra »same same but different«, this ring series from the »reworked« collection utilizes an identical manufacturing process that paradoxically yields a unique artifact every time. Through the unpredictable nature of compression, the metal develops intricate folds during production, giving the hard industrial rings a soft, fabric-like appearance and textile tactility.
